# App icon: "Follow"

Four phrase lines on paper: the ink line (you, at the piano) leads and ends in a point; the three blue lines (the
trio) follow, each a little behind. Ink and blue mean what they mean in the app (you; the strings with you); amber
stays out of the icon so it keeps meaning "stopped / restart here". Chosen 2026-10-29 from four concepts
(https://claude.ai/artifact/Vorq3hXNiEthun3wxWSGij).

`a-follow.svg`, `-dark.svg` and `-tinted.svg` are the sources (1024 x 1024) of the PNGs in
`ios/App/Rehearsal/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset` (rendered, then flattened onto an opaque background).

# Scores from outside, for testing the import

Real MusicXML files from other people, to check that the iPad and the Mac read scores they didn't make. Each is
marked CC0 (public domain dedication) on its MuseScore page; they were taken from the PDMX dataset
([Zenodo 15571083](https://zenodo.org/records/15571083)), which re-exported them with MuseScore 3.6.2.

| File | Piece | Source | Why it's here |
|---|---|---|---|
| `beethoven-op1-1-trio-i.mxl` | Beethoven, Piano Trio Op. 1 No. 1, I | [musescore.com/user/19299806/scores/5956291](https://musescore.com/user/19299806/scores/5956291) | A trio; parts named "Viool" and "Cello"; repeats with an ending |
| `beethoven-kreutzer-i.mxl` | Beethoven, Violin Sonata Op. 47, I (abridged arrangement) | [musescore.com/user/36175072/scores/6367748](https://musescore.com/user/36175072/scores/6367748) | A violin sonata; the violin plays 6 bars alone first; MuseScore's "Title"/"Composer" placeholders left in |
| `picard-cello-sonata2-i.mxl` | D. Picard, Cello Sonata No. 2, I | [musescore.com/user/33612900/scores/5881552](https://musescore.com/user/33612900/scores/5881552) | A cello sonata; the part named "Violoncelle" |
